## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Multiple intermediaries can reduce the share of the final price received by farmers while increasing the price paid by consumers. Small farmers may also struggle to reach enough buyers and arrange affordable transport.

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1. Farmers create listings with crop, quantity, grade, price and pickup area.
2. Buyers browse listings and place individual or group orders.
3. Nearby buyers can combine demand for the same product.
4. A logistics module groups compatible deliveries and suggests a route.
5. A price assistant displays a suggested range with the factors used.
6. A demand assistant estimates likely demand from historical order data.

## Technical Approach
- Backend: Python 3 and Django
- Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript
- Initial database: SQLite; PostgreSQL for deployment
- Analytics: pandas and scikit-learn (optional prototype phase)
- Map data: OpenStreetMap
- Version control: Git and GitHub

## Feasibility and Viability
1.Technical & Economic Feasibility: The system can be developed at a low initial cost using freely available tools and technologies.
2.Operational Feasibility: A simple Tamil and English interface helps farmers and buyers use the platform easily.
3.Financial Viability: Direct connections between farmers and buyers can reduce intermediary costs and provide better earning opportunities for farmers.
4.Social & Market Viability: It supports farmers, local businesses, households, supermarkets and hotels by providing direct access to agricultural products.
5.Environmental Viability: Group orders and shared transportation can reduce delivery trips and fuel consumption.
6.Future Viability: AI-based price suggestions, demand forecasting, online payments and smart delivery management can be added.

## Impact and Benefits
1.Increased Farmer Income: Helps farmers get a better share of the selling price through direct sales.
2.Affordable Prices for Consumers: Provides fresh agricultural products at competitive prices.
3.Reduced Transportation Costs: Group orders and shared delivery help reduce transport expenses.
4.Better Market Access: Connects farmers with households, supermarkets, hotels and local shops.
5.Reduced Food Waste: Demand forecasting helps farmers plan production and sales more effectively.
